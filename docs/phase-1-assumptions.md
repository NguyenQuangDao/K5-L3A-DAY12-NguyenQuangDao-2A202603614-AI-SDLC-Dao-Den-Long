# Phase 1 assumptions

- Night 1 has 300 seconds; no external ticket changes this in Phase 1.
- Carry capacity is 5 and lantern cost is 5.
- Player speed is 5 m/s.
- Pickup and interaction distances use XZ center/foot points only.
- Hitting a shade removes half of carried motes, rounded down, and one flame.
- Invulnerability lasts 1 second.
- A lit lantern creates a 2 m safe radius.
- Pulse costs 1 flame, stuns for 3 seconds, pushes 2 m, and cools down 3 seconds.
- Falling is disabled.
- Default motes are 5 on the island; random spawning is disabled in headless unless a future ticket changes it.
- No requirement in Phase 1 names a jump height or fall penalty, so jump and fall remain disabled and no fall event is emitted.
